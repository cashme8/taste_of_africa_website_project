Latest Food Trends

Ancient Grains Go Global

12 October 2026

Grains first farmed in Africa, like fonio, teff and sorghum, are now sold well beyond the continent. Cooks like them because they are naturally gluten-free and quick to prepare.

Spice Blends in Every Pantry

28 September 2026

Ethiopian berbere, North African harissa and Nigerian suya spice are turning up in home kitchens and restaurants worldwide, bringing bold, layered heat to everyday cooking.

Plant-Based, the African Way

3 September 2026

Many traditional African meals were plant-based long before it was a trend. Leafy stews like Rwandan isombe, bean dishes and lentil misir wat are winning new fans looking for hearty meat-free food.
